Raw Material Analysis & Quality Control

Understanding Materials. Confirming Quality.

XRD, FTIR and PSD analysis for the identification, characterization and quality verification of raw materials — supporting research, industrial production and material sourcing decisions.

Explore Capabilities
Analytical Capability

Know exactly what your material is made of.

Raw materials rarely behave the way a datasheet suggests. Crystalline phase, chemical composition and particle size all shape how a material performs in production — and each of them can shift between suppliers, deposits and batches.

AXION Global brings XRD, FTIR and PSD analysis together so that identification, composition and physical profiling can be read as one picture rather than three disconnected results.

Our Services

Analysis, characterization
& quality verification.

01

X-Ray Diffraction (XRD)

Identification of crystalline phases and mineral content, giving a clear picture of what a material actually contains.

02

Quantitative Phase Analysis

Relative proportions of the identified phases, so major constituents and secondary content can be separated with confidence.

03

FTIR Spectroscopy

Infrared analysis for functional groups and chemical structure — useful where composition matters as much as mineralogy.

04

Particle Size Distribution

PSD profiling to characterize fineness and size spread, key to handling, reactivity and downstream processing behaviour.

05

Raw Material Quality Control

Verification of incoming materials and batch-to-batch consistency checks to support sourcing and production decisions.

06

Reporting & Consultation

Interpretation of results and custom reporting structured around your application, specification or approval requirement.
